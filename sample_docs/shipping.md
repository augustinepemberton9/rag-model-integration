# Shipping

Example Outfitters is a fictional store used to demonstrate retrieval.

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the EU and the US and is free on orders over 60 EUR. Express shipping costs 12 EUR and delivers the next business day for orders placed before 14:00 CET.

Orders ship from the Rotterdam warehouse on weekdays. Orders placed on Saturday or Sunday ship on Monday.

Tracking links are emailed when the parcel leaves the warehouse. A parcel that shows no movement for 7 days is investigated with the carrier; if it is lost, the order is re-sent free of charge.
