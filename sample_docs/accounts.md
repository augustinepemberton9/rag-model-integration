# Accounts and payments

Example Outfitters is a fictional store used to demonstrate retrieval.

Accepted payment methods are Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and iDEAL. Invoices are available as PDF in the account under "Orders".

Business customers can request net-30 payment terms after three paid orders. Prices include VAT for EU deliveries; US prices exclude sales tax, which is added at checkout.

To delete an account, contact support from the account's email address. Order history is kept for 7 years for tax reasons even after deletion.
