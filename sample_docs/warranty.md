# Warranty

Example Outfitters is a fictional store used to demonstrate retrieval.

Tents, backpacks and sleeping bags carry a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Footwear carries a 1-year warranty. Normal wear, misuse and damage from fire or sharp objects are not covered.

To claim, send photos of the defect and the order number through the support form. Approved claims are repaired first; if a repair is not possible, the item is replaced or refunded at its purchase price.

Repairs take about 15 business days including return shipping.
