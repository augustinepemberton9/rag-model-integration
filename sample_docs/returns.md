# Returns and refunds

Example Outfitters is a fictional store used to demonstrate retrieval.

Physical orders can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in the original packaging. Final-sale items, gift cards and personalised products cannot be returned.

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after the warehouse receives the return. Original shipping costs are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged or the wrong item was sent.

To start a return, open the order in your account and choose "Return items". A prepaid label is emailed within one hour. Returns from outside the EU and the US are shipped at the customer's cost.
